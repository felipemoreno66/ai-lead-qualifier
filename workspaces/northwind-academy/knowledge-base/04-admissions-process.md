# Admissions process

Joining Northwind Academy takes four steps. There is no entrance exam and no programming experience is required for the Data & AI Course or the Full Stack Bootcamp.

## Step 1: apply online

Fill in the application form on the website. We ask for your name, email, country, the program you are interested in, your career goal, your budget and how many hours per week you can study.

## Step 2: review

The admissions team reviews your application. An AI system helps prioritize applications and draft replies, and a person reviews every message before it is sent to you. We use your data only to evaluate your application and contact you about it.

## Step 3: admissions call

We invite suitable applicants to a 15-minute admissions call. In the call we check that the program matches your goal, your weekly availability and your budget, and we explain payment plans.

## Step 4: enroll

After the call you receive the enrollment details by email. You confirm your place by making the first payment of the plan you chose.

## Who we serve

We accept applicants from all countries except sanctioned countries.
