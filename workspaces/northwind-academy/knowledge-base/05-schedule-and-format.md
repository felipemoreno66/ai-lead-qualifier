# Schedule and format

## How classes work

Programs are taught online. The Full Stack Bootcamp and the Data & AI Course run as live cohorts, so you learn together with other students and a teacher answers your questions in real time. Sessions are recorded for students who miss one.

## Time zone

Our schedule uses Colombia time (America/Bogota). Live sessions are held in the evening so people with daytime jobs can attend.

## Weekly workload

| Program | Duration | Weekly effort |
|---|---|---|
| Full Stack Bootcamp | 16 weeks | about 20 hours |
| Data & AI Course | 10 weeks | about 10 hours |
| 1:1 Mentorship | monthly, cancel anytime | one 60-minute session per week, plus your own practice |

The weekly hours include live sessions, exercises and project work.

## Mentorship scheduling

You agree the weekly 60-minute session time with your mentor. If you need to reschedule, tell your mentor at least 24 hours before the session.

## What you need

- A computer with a stable internet connection.
- A webcam and microphone for live sessions.
- Time set aside each week, matching the program you choose.

## Language

Live sessions are taught in English. The admissions team answers in English or Spanish.
