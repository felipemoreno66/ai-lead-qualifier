# Refund policy

## Full Stack Bootcamp and Data & AI Course

- You can ask for a full refund within 14 days after the program starts.
- After 14 days we do not refund payments already made.
- If you chose a payment plan and leave after the first 14 days, you are not charged for the months after your cancellation request, but earlier payments are not returned.

## 1:1 Mentorship

- The mentorship is billed monthly and you can cancel anytime.
- Cancel before your next billing date to avoid the next charge.
- The current month is not refunded once the first session of that month has taken place.
- The 3-month plan can be refunded in full before the first session. After that, the remaining unused months can be refunded.

## How to request a refund

Send an email to admissions@northwind-academy.example with your full name and the program name. Refund requests are answered within 5 business days and a person always reviews them.

## Scholarships

Refunds for scholarship recipients follow the same rules and apply to the amount actually paid.
