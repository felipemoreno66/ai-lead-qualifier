# Authoring notes (not ingested)

Files whose names start with an underscore are skipped by the ingestion script.

All content is fictional and must stay consistent with `../workspace.yaml` (prices, payment plans, programs, durations, link domains).

Topics deliberately NOT covered, so that "not in the documentation" answers can be tested. Do not add them:

- Job placement or employment guarantees.
- Visa sponsorship or immigration help.
- Accreditation, degrees or certificates recognized by universities.
- Corporate or team pricing.
- Teacher names and biographies.
- Refunds in currencies other than USD.
