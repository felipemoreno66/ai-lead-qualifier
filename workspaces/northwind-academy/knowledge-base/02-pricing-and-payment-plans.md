# Pricing and payment plans

All prices are in US dollars (USD). Prices are the same for every country we serve.

## Full Stack Bootcamp pricing

The Full Stack Bootcamp costs USD 4,900 when paid in full. Instead of paying in full you can choose a payment plan of 6 monthly payments of USD 890.

## Data & AI Course pricing

The Data & AI Course costs USD 2,400 when paid in full. The payment plan is 3 monthly payments of USD 850.

## 1:1 Mentorship pricing

The 1:1 Mentorship costs USD 190 per month and you can cancel anytime. A 3-month plan is available for USD 520.

## Payment plans

- Payment plans are available for the Full Stack Bootcamp and the Data & AI Course.
- The first payment is due at enrollment and the following payments are charged monthly.
- Mentorship is billed monthly, or as the 3-month plan.
- Plan options are confirmed by the admissions team before you enroll, so you always know the exact amounts first.

## Discounts

We do not publish promotional discounts. Financial help is handled through scholarships, described in the scholarships document. Any price or plan not listed here is not available.
