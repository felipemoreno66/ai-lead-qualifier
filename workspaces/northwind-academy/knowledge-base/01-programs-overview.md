# Northwind Academy programs

Northwind Academy is a fictional online school for career changers who want to start working in technology. We offer three programs. This page compares them; each has its own section below.

## Program comparison

| Program | Format | Duration | Weekly effort | Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full Stack Bootcamp | Live online cohort | 16 weeks | about 20 hours | 4,900 |
| Data & AI Course | Live online, part-time | 10 weeks | about 10 hours | 2,400 |
| 1:1 Mentorship | Personal sessions | Monthly, cancel anytime | 60-minute weekly session | 190 per month |

## Full Stack Bootcamp

The Full Stack Bootcamp is a 16-week live online program with about 20 hours of work per week. You learn JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Node.js and PostgreSQL by building projects that end up in your portfolio. It suits people who can dedicate most of their weekly time to learning and want to build complete web applications.

### What you learn

- JavaScript and TypeScript fundamentals.
- Front-end development with React.
- Back-end development with Node.js.
- Relational databases with PostgreSQL.
- Working with Git and code reviews.

### Who it is for

Career changers who want a structured, intensive path into web development. No previous professional programming experience is required, but you should be ready to commit about 20 hours every week for 16 weeks.

## Data & AI Course

The Data & AI Course is a 10-week part-time course with about 10 hours of work per week. It covers Python, SQL, pandas, machine learning basics and building applications that use large language models (LLMs).

### What you learn

- Python for data work.
- SQL queries and data modelling basics.
- Data analysis with pandas.
- Machine learning basics.
- Building simple LLM applications.

### Who it is for

People who want to move into data or applied AI while keeping a job or other commitments, because the weekly load is about half of the bootcamp.

## 1:1 Mentorship

The 1:1 Mentorship pairs you with a personal mentor for a weekly 60-minute session. Sessions focus on your portfolio and interview preparation, and the plan is monthly so you can cancel anytime.

### Who it is for

Self-taught learners or graduates of other programs who already know the basics and want personal guidance, feedback on their projects and practice for interviews.

## Not sure which program fits?

Tell us your goal and how many hours per week you can study. The admissions team recommends one program and, if useful, a payment plan. You can [book a 15-minute admissions call](https://northwind-academy.example/admissions-call) or write to admissions@northwind-academy.example.
