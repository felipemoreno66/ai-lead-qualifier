# Scholarships

Northwind Academy offers a limited number of scholarships each cohort for applicants who could not otherwise join a program.

## Who can apply

- Anyone who applies to the Full Stack Bootcamp or the Data & AI Course.
- Applicants who explain how the program supports their career change and why they need financial help.

Scholarships are not available for the 1:1 Mentorship.

## How to apply

1. Submit the regular application form and mention that you want to be considered for a scholarship.
2. The admissions team asks for a short written statement about your goals and your situation.
3. A review committee decides. You receive an answer by email.

## What to know

- Scholarships are limited and are not guaranteed.
- The amount is decided case by case and is not published in advance.
- Applying for a scholarship does not affect whether you are accepted into a program.
- Scholarship decisions are made by people at the academy, never automatically.

## Questions

Write to admissions@northwind-academy.example or [book a 15-minute admissions call](https://northwind-academy.example/admissions-call).
