# Frequently asked questions

## General

### Do I need previous programming experience?

No. The Full Stack Bootcamp and the Data & AI Course start from the basics. The 1:1 Mentorship is better if you already know some programming.

### How many hours per week do I need?

About 20 hours per week for the Full Stack Bootcamp, about 10 hours per week for the Data & AI Course, and one 60-minute session per week plus your own practice for the 1:1 Mentorship.

### Which program should I choose?

Choose the Full Stack Bootcamp if you want to build web applications and can study about 20 hours a week. Choose the Data & AI Course if you prefer data and AI and have about 10 hours a week. Choose the 1:1 Mentorship if you want personal feedback on your portfolio and interview practice.

## Payments

### Can I pay in installments?

Yes. The Full Stack Bootcamp can be paid in 6 monthly payments of USD 890 and the Data & AI Course in 3 monthly payments of USD 850.

### What does the mentorship cost?

USD 190 per month, or USD 520 for the 3-month plan. You can cancel anytime.

### Is there financial help?

We have a limited number of scholarships for the bootcamp and the course. See the scholarships document for how to apply.

## Admissions

### How do I apply?

Fill in the online application form. The admissions team then replies by email and may invite you to a 15-minute admissions call.

### From which countries can I apply?

From all countries except sanctioned countries.

### Who reads my application?

An AI system helps prioritize and draft replies, and a person reviews every message before it is sent.

## Programs

### Are classes live or recorded?

The bootcamp and the course are live. Sessions are recorded for students who miss one.

### Can I switch from the Data & AI Course to the Bootcamp?

Ask the admissions team. Switching is decided case by case.
