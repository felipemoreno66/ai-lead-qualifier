AI Lead Qualifier: three design directions

Fictional Northwind Academy data. Each option has seven screens: public form, form success, leads list, lead detail (review the draft), lead detail (error with Retry), knowledge-base chat, metrics. Open on your phone and on desktop.

Option A: Ledger

Cool paper, ink and forest green, with a grotesque for text and mono only for figures. A top bar and a dense, ruled table. The lead detail reads like a document: the draft letter sits in a bordered sheet at left, the reasons in a ruled column at right, the run log below. Calm, editorial, closest to a trusted back-office tool.

Option B: Console

Dark graphite with an amber signal color, IBM Plex Sans and Plex Mono. Sidebar navigation, status chips, and a three-pane lead detail: lead rail, qualification and data in the middle, and a fixed review inspector on the right holding the draft and Approve / Edit / Reject. Built for high-volume triage with the keyboard.

Option C: Warm Queue

Soft lavender-gray, plum and terracotta with a serif voice (Young Serif) over Figtree. Leads are grouped by priority (needs attention, hot, warm, still scoring, cold) instead of one long table. The detail is a single readable column that starts with the draft reply as a large letter card. The form is three short sections with pill choices, and mobile gets a bottom tab bar. Friendly and spacious, best for phones.
